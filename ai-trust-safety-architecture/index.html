AI Trust & Safety Enterprise Architecture

A layered reference architecture for building AI systems that are secure, safe and trustworthy. Every component is mapped to MITRE ATLAS techniques and mitigations, to ISO/IEC 23894 risk management, and to ISO/IEC TR 24028 trustworthiness. Score your own implementation to see which adversary techniques you are exposed to.

1 · Layered reference architecture

The architecture has nine layers across three planes. The data plane carries every request, from identity through input trust, agents, models and knowledge to output trust. The control plane governs it. The assurance plane watches, tests and responds. Click any component to see its MITRE ATLAS techniques and mitigations and its standards mapping. The number on each component is how many ATLAS techniques it helps counter.
